STEP 11 — Run Quality Control Final response se pehle complete checklist run karo. ## STEP 12 — Deliver Clean Output Output copy-paste-ready rakho. Scale anchor: [specific familiar object] remains clearly visible and is massively dwarfed by the giant. Specific Change Command: **CHANGE ONLY [DETAIL]** Only requested detail change karo. Everything else preserve karo. ### 6.
